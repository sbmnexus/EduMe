EduMe

Smart Study Planner for Students 📚

EduMe is a modern study-planning platform designed to help students organize their studies, manage tasks, track study time, and stay focused on their goals.

✨ Features

- 📅 Study Planner — Organize and manage daily study tasks
- ⏱️ Study Timer — Track focused study sessions
- 📝 Quiz — Practice and test your knowledge
- 📊 Progress & Reports — Monitor study progress and performance
- 🎨 Themes — Personalized light and dark experience
- 📱 Responsive Design — Works across mobile, tablet, and desktop
- ⚡ PWA Support — Installable web-app experience
- 🚀 Performance & Bug Fixes — Continuous improvements for a smoother experience
